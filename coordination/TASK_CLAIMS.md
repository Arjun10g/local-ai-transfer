State semantics: source implementation, evidence, and gate approval are
separate. `MERGED_SOURCE_PENDING_GATE` means source is on `main` only; it is
not a release or live-readiness approval. `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT` and
`REJECTED_REPAIR_PENDING` are not approval states. `SUPERSEDED_SOURCE_HISTORY`
retains a historical task without making it current authority;
`BLOCKED_BELOW_GATE` records evidence that failed or cannot satisfy its gate;
`QUARANTINED` means source is present on `main` but unreachable/disabled
pending repair — it is not an approval state and confers no capability.
`CHANGES_REQUESTED` records a slice whose independent review returned
ACCEPT_WITH_REQUIRED_FIXES; the notes name the branch carrying the fixes,
and it returns to review rather than to approval.
`DONE_OPERATOR_ACTION` (defined 2026-09-11, refresh v6) records an
operator/Sol action completed **outside source** — an environment, ledger,
filesystem, or provider-account change that no commit can carry. It is **not a
gate approval** and confers no capability: it states only that the named action
was performed and is verifiable in the tree or the environment. A task in this
state may still leave residual source defects open, and those must be named in
its notes rather than treated as closed by the action.
