# Risk Register

Ratings: probability and impact are `Low`, `Medium`, or `High`. Sol updates them at each phase gate.

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Trigger / evidence | Primary owner | Mitigation | Contingency / gate |

| R-002 | Portable native binary or vendored source is not approved | Medium | High | Security/license gate rejected or delayed | Luna D + Sol | Early SBOM, license notices, static analysis, scans, reproducible build | Use an approved prebuilt internal runtime only through a new ADR; do not install public software |

## Risk closure rule

A risk is closed only with evidence, not because the team expects it to be fine. Closed risks remain in history with the closing artifact and build ID.
